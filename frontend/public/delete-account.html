Delete your account & data

Parakh (developer: SKDev) · Last updated 3 July 2026

This page explains how to delete your Parakh account and the data associated with it. You can delete individual items yourself inside the app, or request full deletion of your account and all associated data by email.

Delete individual items in the app

  • Open the Today tab and tap the 🗑 (trash) icon next to any logged food to remove that entry immediately.
  • Edit your profile and targets any time from the profile / targets screen.

Delete your entire account and all data

Email us from the email address associated with your Parakh account:

rsumit123@gmail.com
  1. Send an email with the subject "Delete my Parakh account".
  2. Send it from the same Google account email you use to sign in to Parakh (this lets us verify the request).
  3. We confirm and permanently delete your account and associated data within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Account info (email, Google account ID, name, profile picture)Deleted
Food log entries (foods, portions, nutrition, dates)Deleted
Profile & targets (sex, age, weight, activity, goals, custom targets)Deleted
Meal photos used for estimatesNot stored — processed transiently and never retained

We do not retain your personal data after deletion, except where a short additional period is required to remove it from routine backups (no more than 90 days), or where retention is required by law.

Public product catalog

Parakh's catalog of packaged products and their health scores is general reference information, not personal data, and is unaffected by account deletion.
